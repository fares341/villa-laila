أسئلة شائعة قبل الحجز.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is من 15:00 and check-out is حتى 11:00. The front desk is open 08:00–22:00, and استقبال واتساب answers 24/7.

Is breakfast included?

الإفطار الصباحي is served every morning from 8 to 11, with vegetarian, vegan and halal options.

Can you arrange airport pickup?

Yes. Message reception on WhatsApp with your flight number and arrival time.

Are there women-only dorms?

Yes, an 8-bed and a 6-bed women-only dorm, plus men-only dorms and one mixed dorm.

How do I book a tour?

استفسر عبر واتساب. Tours run daily and reception confirms times and the price right away.
